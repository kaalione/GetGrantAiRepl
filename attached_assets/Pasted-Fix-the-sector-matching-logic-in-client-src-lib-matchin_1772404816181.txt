Fix the sector matching logic in client/src/lib/matching.ts.

PROBLEM: A company with industry "Teknik & IT" gets full sector match points 
for grants where 'tech' appears as ONE of many unrelated sectors. Example: 
a grant about agriculture+food+education+tech scores identically to a pure 
tech grant — because the current code does sectors.some(s => match) which 
passes on ANY single match.

ROOT CAUSE: The matching is all-or-nothing. One matching sector out of 4+ 
gives the same score as 4 matching sectors out of 4.

FIX: Replace the sector scoring block with a ratio-based approach:

1. Count how many of the grant's sectors match the company industry
2. Calculate matchRatio = matchingCount / totalSectors
3. Score based on ratio:
   - matchRatio >= 0.5 (majority match): score = Math.round(20 * matchRatio)
   - matchRatio > 0 AND matchCount === 1 AND totalSectors >= 3 (incidental 'tech'): score = 2
   - matchRatio > 0 AND partial: score = 8
   - matchRatio === 0 (zero match): score = -15 (penalty, same as before)
   - No sectors / empty / 'all': score = 6 (neutral, same as before)

4. When expanding company industry to match against sectors, handle synonyms:
   - "Teknik & IT" should match: tech, it, digital, software, saas, ai, data
   - "Hälsa" should match: health, life_science, medtech
   - "Energi" should match: energy, cleantech, climate
   - "Jordbruk"/"Livsmedel" should match: agriculture, food, agtech

EXPECTED RESULT after fix:

- EU grant with sectors [manufacturing, aerospace, tech, energy, maritime, transport]: score = 2 (was 20)  
- Vinnova grant with sectors [tech, ai, innovation]: score = 20 (unchanged)
- Both irrelevant EU grants drop ~18 points and fall out of top 10 for a SaaS company
